## chords-db

The ukulele and guitar chord-shape voicings in
`packages/models/lib/src/chordpro/chord_shapes_data.dart` are generated from the
[tombatossals/chords-db](https://github.com/tombatossals/chords-db) dataset
(pinned commit `df06fa7b425cf5fd29485ff6591236b3557e3fac`). The raw dataset is
vendored under `packages/models/tool/chords_db/`.
