Joe's Tabs - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Joe's Tabs is a chord-sheet and tuner app for ukulele and guitar, available on the web and on Android. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Using the app without an account

You can browse songs, chords and the tuner without signing in. Your settings and saved songs are then stored only on your device.

Accounts

If you create an account, the app stores:

  • Your email address, used to sign you in.
  • If you use "Continue with Google": the basic profile Google shares with the app (your name and email address).
  • The list of songs you save to your favorites, so they sync between devices.

This data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and is used only to run the app. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Microphone

The tuner uses your microphone to detect the pitch of your instrument. The audio is processed on your device and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Ads

The Android app can show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob when you choose to support the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and all data linked to it, email tabsxlabs@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Joe's Tabs account". Your account, email address and saved favorites are deleted within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: tabsxlabs@gmail.com.
